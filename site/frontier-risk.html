AgentGuard · demo 2 · FLI AI Safety Index rules

Safety rules from the FLI Index, enforced and proved.

The FLI AI Safety Index and the benchmarks it uses list 388 safety rules. 40 of them are about what an agent is allowed to do. AgentGuard turns 33 of those into a ready-made rule template, and Lean proves the template keeps working whatever rules you add after it.

03 — The proof

What is proved about the template

Each guarantee below holds for every list of rules placed after the template, every sequence of actions and every person answering the approvals, including one who approves everything.

GuaranteeIn plain wordsStatus
Later rules can't override it

If the template catches an action, no rule after it, not even "allow everything", gets to decide.

proved
Deny means it never runs

An action caught by one of the template's deny rules never runs.

proved
Ask means a person

An action caught by any rule of the template is never allowed automatically, and runs only if a person approves it.

proved
One theorem per rule

Each FLI and AIR-Bench rule has its own theorem, so a reviewer can check them one by one.

proved
Budgets and a kill switch

Limits on power-seeking actions (CAIS Machiavelli) and a stop button (FLI Index). They change the rule language, so their statements are waiting for review.

proposed

The proofs build on AgentGuard's existing ten theorems and change none of its definitions. Like them, they are machine-checked in Lean 4 with standard axioms only.

04 — Limits

What the template does not do

Actions, not wordsThe template stops an agent from scanning a network. It does not stop a model from explaining how to; that needs a classifier or a person.
Tool names are placeholdersA rule only catches the tools its pattern matches. A scanner called nmap.run needs its own pattern.
Our reading of the rulesWhich rules fit, and how they are worded, is the Beneficial AI Foundation's interpretation, not FLI's or the benchmark authors'.
Fictional storiesThe demo's agents, tools and people are made up. It runs in your browser and calls nothing.
