# Security

AgentGuard is a research prototype. Don't rely on it to protect production systems yet.

To report a vulnerability, please contact the maintainers privately through GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" button on this repository rather than opening a public issue.

What is proved is listed in [`docs/proofs.md`](docs/proofs.md); what is trusted rather than proved, and the known limits, in [`docs/threat-model.md`](docs/threat-model.md).
